## Block 2.1 · Case study: reorganising the service portal CampusNow · JUDGED · Core · about 15 min

**Draws on:** A1 · Seeing an interface, and designing from the user's side · A2 · How people scan, select and prioritise · A3 · What guides the eye · A4 · How much at once · A5 · Recognition instead of search, clarity instead of overload, and weighing a measure.

An education organisation runs CampusNow, a digital portal for course booking, certificates, dates and learning materials. All the content is there, but users find many functions only with difficulty. Complaints are rising, abandonment in the booking process is increasing, and mobile use is especially problematic. The observed problems: the start page is overloaded with information, the navigation items are named after the internal organisation, important functions are not visually prioritised, different page types use different structures, the text blocks are long and hard to scan, and users often have to search instead of being guided directly. The conditions: a budget of €150,000, 14 weeks for the implementation, no complete relaunch, first improvements have to be visible quickly, and marketing asks for a modern look, support for fewer questions and IT for standardisation. You work through six steps.

**Question 1 of 6 · Analyse the central perception and structure problems of the portal**

*How to build the answer:*

- Open CampusNow and book a course as a learner would, once on the desktop and once on the phone.
- Write one problem per line as an effect on the user: what the portal makes hard, not only what is wrong with it.

Write them as a list, one problem per line, each as an effect on the user. At least five.

**Question 3 of 6 · Develop 4 prioritised user-centred measures**

*How to build the answer:*

- Turn each group of problems into something you could change within 14 weeks and without a relaunch.

**Question 4 of 6 · Rate your measures on effect on users, effort, risk and timeliness**

Write a few words for each of your four measures that cover all four criteria. Effort means time and people, not money. Many teams rate each Low, Medium or High with a reason.

*How to build the answer:*

- Take your measures in the order of question 3.
- For each criterion, say how the measure does and why; effort is the time and the people it takes.
- Let the ratings check your order: if a later measure rates better, say why it still comes later, or change the order.

**Question 5 of 6 · Make a reasoned prioritisation decision for the first 14 weeks**

Say what you do in the first 14 weeks, in which order, and what you leave for later, with the reason.

*How to build the answer:*

- Use your ratings: what shows quickly and helps most comes first.
- Say what you start now but finish later, and what waits.
- Keep the conditions in mind: no relaunch, first improvements visible quickly, marketing, support and IT.

**Question 6 of 6 · Formulate a recommendation to the division management**

Three to five sentences: what you recommend, what you do not, and what you expect to change.

*How to build the answer:*

- Lead with the recommendation.
- Say what you do not prioritise and why.
- Say what you expect to change for users and for the organisation.
