## Block 2.1 · Case study: redesigning SafeHome Online · JUDGED · Core · about 15 min

**Draws on:** A1 · UX, UI and the psychology behind them · A2 · How people see · A3 · Expectations and habits, and three aims of an interface · A4 · Feelings that work before thinking · A5 · Weighing a design measure.

An insurance company runs SafeHome Online, a self-service portal for damage reports, contract overviews and document downloads. It is judged complete in content but has massive usage problems: 46 % of damage reports are abandoned, many customers call the service centre although the functions exist online, users call the portal “complicated”, “cold” and “unsafe”, the specialist departments want to add more notices and safeguards, and the management wants a modern, trustworthy and more efficient customer experience. The current portal: very many blocks of text and legal notices on the entry page, primary actions that are not visually emphasised, no visible progress in the process, warning colours used often, even for neutral information, an upload that is formally correct but worded in an off-putting way, and important actions partly placed below long texts. The conditions: €180,000 and 12 weeks for a first optimisation, the legal notices must stay, IT can only intervene to a limited extent at short notice, and there is no full relaunch, so the measures must be targeted. You carry out a simplified psychological UX analysis in five steps.

**Question 1 of 5 · Identify at least 5 central effect problems in the current portal**

*How to build the answer:*

- Open SafeHome Online and start a damage report as a customer would.
- Write one problem per line as an effect: what the design does to the customer, not only what is wrong with it.

Write them as a list, one problem per line, each as an effect on the customer.

**Question 3 of 5 · Develop 4 prioritised improvement measures**

*How to build the answer:*

- Turn each group of problems into something you could change within 12 weeks and with little IT capacity.
- Put them in order: the measure that helps customers most within the conditions first.

Write them as a numbered list, the first priority first, each with what it changes for customers.

[[ANSWER lines=6]]

**Question 4 of 5 · Justify your prioritisation using the criteria effect on users, feasibility, risk and time pressure**

Use all four criteria and say how they support your order.

*How to build the answer:*

- Say for your first measure what it changes for customers and why it is feasible with limited IT.
- Say what risk each carries and how the 12 weeks shape the order.
- Keep the legal notices in mind: they stay, but they can be placed and worded differently.

[[ANSWER lines=5]]

**Question 5 of 5 · Formulate a short recommendation to the management**

Three to five sentences: what you recommend, what you do not, and what you expect to change.

*How to build the answer:*

- Lead with the recommendation.
- Give the one reason that will carry the management.
- Say what you expect to change.
